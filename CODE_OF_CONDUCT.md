# Code of Conduct

FollowUp follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Its full text applies to everyone taking part in this project: in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's experience, background or identity.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; criticise ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, trolling, sexualised content, or publishing others' private information.
- Assume good intent, and help newcomers.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour breaks this code, report it privately to the maintainer through GitHub: use the **Report content** option on the comment or issue, or [GitHub's abuse report form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse). Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits or other contributions that don't follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful.
